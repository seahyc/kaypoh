I run 5 coding agents at once and kept losing track of which one was waiting on me.

So I made my notifications take me to the exact pane, and gave them a tiger mum.

[image: tiger mum notice "Claude done. Why not faster?"]

2/
Click → the exact iTerm2 split / tmux pane / VS Code window comes forward. Or type a reply in
the notice and it goes straight to the agent.

[video: notice → click → pane]

3/
The body is what the agent actually said, not "waiting for input". Short turns don't ping you,
and each session updates one notice instead of stacking ten.
